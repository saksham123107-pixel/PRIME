## Description:

A Chinese-language agent for lawful, public-source technical due diligence and product analysis across SaaS, AI agent systems, mobile apps, open-source projects, API services, and related digital products.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, founders, investors, and product teams use this agent to analyze publicly observable product behavior, technology stacks, API patterns, architecture signals, and technical-commercial consistency before deeper due diligence or competitive review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The analysis workflow could be misused for unauthorized probing, bypassing access controls, credential abuse, or attempts to extract private internals.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for lawful analysis of public information or systems the user is authorized to assess; do not use it to bypass access controls or obtain private data.

Risk: Reverse-engineering reports can contain inferred backend, API, architecture, or prompt conclusions that may be wrong or incomplete.

Mitigation: Require evidence labels, confidence ratings, and human review before acting on findings or making business, investment, or compliance decisions.
